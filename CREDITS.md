# Asset credits

The staged realism work tracks its [asset inventory and preparation records](docs/room-review/assets.md).
Prepared runtime files, original authorship, checksums, and retired assets are recorded in the inventory.

## 3D models — `public/models/*.glb`

The retained keyboard, mouse, laptop, books and lamps come from the **Furniture Kit** by [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit),
licensed **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain — attribution appreciated, not required).

Downloaded 2026-08-24 from kenney.nl (`kenney_furniture-kit.zip`, GLTF format folder).

## Realistic room — local models, textures and environment

[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license) assets are CC0: [Wood Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor), [Plastered Wall](https://polyhaven.com/a/plastered_wall), [Fabric Pattern 07](https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_pattern_07) normal/roughness maps, [Studio Small 09](https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09) HDR, [Modern Arm Chair 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/modern_arm_chair_01), and [Potted Plant 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/potted_plant_02). Local variants use 1K maps and decoder-free GLBs with same-origin external images.

The desk, office chair, tables, shelf, rugs, cushion and coat rack in `public/models/realistic/` are original procedural assets generated for this project by `scripts/prepare-studio-furniture.mjs`, dedicated to **CC0 1.0**. Their generator and preparation records remain available for reproduction. Interactive housings and trim are authored in the application source under the repository license.
